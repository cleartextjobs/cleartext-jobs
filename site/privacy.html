Cleartext Jobs / IT, Europe

Privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What this site does

Cleartext Jobs lists IT jobs in Europe, collected automatically from the public job boards of the companies that post them. Each listing links to the original posting, where you apply directly with the company.

Personal data

The site has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms and no CV upload. It does not receive applications and does not store personal data about visitors.

Cookies and visit counts

The site sets no cookies. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or local storage and does not track individual visitors across sites. It records page views, the referring website, country and device type in aggregate. Your filter choices are remembered only in your own browser.

Social media

The Cleartext Jobs Facebook Page publishes selections of jobs from this site. Posting is done by an automated routine owned by Cleartext Jobs, which only publishes to that Page and reads no data about people.

Contact and removal requests

Companies that want a listing corrected or removed, and anyone with a privacy question, can write to cleartextjobs@gmail.com.
